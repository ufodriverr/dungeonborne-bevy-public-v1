# Release notes

## 0.8.0

The first public release. Not finished, but playable from the title screen through full runs.

- The Crypt and the Gothic Castle, both floors each, streamed in cells, with lights, traps, doors,
  chests, altars, destroyable props and extraction portals; the Test Arena with every monster,
  every weapon and class switching.
- All eight classes with their Q/E skills, weapon combos, heavy attacks, blocking, staffs,
  crossbows and second weapon sets; skill effects and sounds from the game data.
- Monsters with their behaviour trees, attacks, summons and totems; bosses including the
  Werewolf, Reaper, Cyclops, Wendigo, Shadow King, Khazra, Executioner, Beholder and Cursed King.
- Items with affixes, potions, flasks, torches, loot, the stash, the merchant and gold.
- Settings: resolution, window mode, quality presets, field of view, sensitivity, volumes, key
  rebinding.
- Known gaps: single-player only (co-op is planned); some skills use a stand-in third-person body;
  a few effects and sounds aren't right yet; values the game data does not give are guesses,
  marked `HYPOTHESIS` in the code. There is no published extractor yet; see EXTRACTION.md.
